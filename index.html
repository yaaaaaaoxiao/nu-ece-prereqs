    How to use this map

    • Reading the map: each row is a course level (100, 200, 300) and each column a department. Courses sit under their prerequisites, and every link runs downward: from the bottom of a course into the course it unlocks. Solid links are required, dashed ones are one of several options, and faint ones connect other departments' courses.
    • Select a course by clicking it, searching, or picking it from the list. Its prerequisite chain turns violet and the courses it unlocks turn orange.
    • Mark your courses: Shift-click (or ⌘/Ctrl-click) several courses, Shift-drag a box around them, long-press on a touch screen, or press Mark courses; then mark them taken or planned, or put them in a quarter, all at once. A course's details panel can also select it together with everything it requires. Every change can be undone.
    • Colors: blue is your plan (solid for taken, dashed for planned), green means you can take it now, and amber means a planned course needs a check. The number on a course is how many courses on the map it leads to; darker means more.
    • Plan ahead: the Plan tab lays out your quarters, warns when a prerequisite comes too late, and tracks the requirements of your majors and minors together. Your plan is saved only in this browser.
    • Highlight the courses in a degree requirement, a technical track, or a quarter's schedule from the toolbar.
    • Move around: drag to pan, scroll to move, pinch or Ctrl + scroll to zoom.
    • Keyboard: / search, arrow keys move between courses, Enter selects (Shift+Enter adds to a multi-selection), T P N mark taken, planned or not taken, ⌘/Ctrl+Z undoes, Esc clears, + − 0 zoom.
