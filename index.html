      How to use this map

      Read the map

      • Each column is a department. Each row is a prerequisite step: a course sits one row below its last prerequisite. The legend can change the rows to course levels.
      • Links go down, from a course to the courses it unlocks. Solid links are required. Dashed links show one of several options.
      • Blue is your plan: solid for taken, dashed for planned. Green: you can take the course now. Amber: a planned course needs a check.
      • The number on a course counts the courses it leads to. The dark letters in F W S show the quarters it runs this year.

      Use the map

      • See a course: point at it to see its details in the panel. To keep them, click it, search for it, or find it in the list. Its prerequisites turn violet, and the courses it unlocks turn orange.
      • Mark your courses: Shift-click them, Shift-drag a box, or press Mark courses. On a touch screen, long-press a course. You can undo each change.
      • Plan a route: open a course and press Show the path. The path shows the courses you still need, with the soonest quarter for each. Add to my plan adds all of them.
      • Pick your programs with the button next to the title. The map shows all courses in your majors' departments, plus your minors' courses.
      • Hide a course you do not want: open it and press Not interested. The site warns you if you need the course. To see hidden courses again, press the hidden count in the toolbar.
      • Plan your quarters in the Plan tab. It shows prerequisite problems and checks your program requirements. Your plan stays in this browser.
      • Highlight a requirement list, a track, or a quarter's courses with the menu in the toolbar.
      • Move around: drag or scroll. To zoom, pinch or use Ctrl + scroll. Zoom in to see course names.

      Keyboard

      • / Search
      • ←↑→↓ Move between courses
      • Enter Open a course
      • Shift+Enter Add to the selection
      • T P N Taken, planned, not taken
      • X Not interested
      • ⌘/Ctrl+Z Undo
      • Esc Close or clear
      • + − 0 Zoom in, out, fit
