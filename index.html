      How to use this map

      Read the map

      • Related courses sit side by side: each course is placed under its prerequisites. Each row is a prerequisite step: a course sits one row below its last prerequisite.
      • On a degree map, the courses every student takes are on top. Electives are in labeled boxes below, one per track or area, and choices with no prerequisites are small one-line chips. A course that several courses in a box need sends one line to the box, which splits inside it.
      • The legend can change the rows to course levels, or the columns to departments (one block of columns each).
      • Links go down, from a course to the courses it unlocks. Solid links are required. Dashed links show one of several options.
      • Blue is your plan: solid for taken, dashed for planned. Green: you can take the course now. Amber: a planned course needs a check.
      • The number on a course counts the courses it leads to. The dark letters in F W S show the quarters it runs this year.

      Find a course or a major

      • Search on the home page finds a course by number or title. Its page shows the courses it unlocks and what it requires, two layers back. Prerequisites above the graph shows fewer or all of them. A fading line and +3 on a course mean it needs 3 more; click +3 to show them.
      • Drag the edge of the details panel to give the graph or the details more room. Double-click it to reset.
      • Pre-req graph on the home page opens a major's map: all its courses and how they connect.

      Use the map

      • See a course: point at it to see its details in the panel. To keep them, click it, search for it, or find it in the list. Its prerequisites turn violet, and the courses it unlocks turn orange.
      • Mark your courses: Shift-click them, Shift-drag a box, or press Mark courses. On a touch screen, long-press a course. You can undo each change.
      • Plan a route: open a course and press Show the path. The path shows the courses you still need, with the soonest quarter for each. Add to my plan adds all of them.
      • Pick your programs with the button next to the title. The map shows their courses and what those require. Turn off Program courses only in the toolbar to see every course in your majors' departments (they're added under Other courses).
      • One of several: where a course accepts any of several (MATH 228-1, MATH 230-1, MATH 290-2 ...), the graph shows the usual one, with cards stacked behind it and the others named on it: or MATH 230-1 +5. The usual one is the one you took or planned, else the one your programs list. To draw them all, open the Legend and turn on Show every option.
      • Hide a course you do not want: open it and press Not interested. The site warns you if you need the course. To see hidden courses again, press the hidden count in the toolbar.
      • Plan your quarters in the Plan tab. It shows prerequisite problems and checks your program requirements. Your plan stays in this browser.
      • Highlight a requirement list, a track, or a quarter's courses with the menu in the toolbar.
      • Move around: drag or scroll. To zoom, pinch or use Ctrl + scroll. Zoom in to see course names.

      Keyboard

      • / Search
      • ←↑→↓ Move between courses
      • Enter Open a course
      • Shift+Enter Add to the selection
      • T P N Taken, planned, not taken
      • X Not interested
      • ⌘/Ctrl+Z Undo
      • Esc Close or clear
      • + − 0 Zoom in, out, fit
